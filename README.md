## Description
> [!NOTE]
> Originally the major selling point of this mod was that it turned the hard-coded `EntityGroup` system into datapack tags.  
> They put this feature in vanilla in 1.20.5 so... this is a normal enchantment mod now?  
> I'm not even upset, I just think it's funny that I did it first.

A modpack utility mod masquerading as an enchantment mod.

An issue with modpacks is that new mobs often don't fall into the vanilla categories of undead, arthropod, and aquatic. ExtraDE adds 5 additional
categories that cover the majority of use-cases.

ExtraDE also adds custom group icons for Provi's Health Bars. Using PHB will also grant new custom health bar portraits.

## New Entity Tags
### Collection Groups
- Humanoid (`extra-damage-enchantments:humanoid`)
  - Receives extra damage from the Rebel enchantment.
  - Consists of human and humanlike mobs (Players, Villagers, Piglins).
- OuterWorldly (`extra-damage-enchantments:outerworldly`)
  - Receives extra damage from the Voidrender enchantment.
  - Consists of _dimensional_ mobs (Endermen, Ender Dragon).
- Arcane (`extra-damage-enchantments:arcane`)
  - Receives extra damage from the Unravel enchantment.
  - Consists of mobs that are _made of magic_ (Allays, Vexes).
- FungiFloral (`extra-damage-enchantments:fungifloral`)
  - Receives extra damage from the Herbicide enchantment.
  - Consists of plantlike and mushroomlike mobs (Mooshrooms, Wardens).
- Beastly (`extra-damage-enchantments:beastly`)
  - Receives extra damage from the Butcher enchantment.
  - Consists of animal and beastlike mobs (Cows, Hoglins, etc).

### Enchantment Vulnerabilities
- `extra-damage-enchantments:sensitive_to_rebel`
- `extra-damage-enchantments:sensitive_to_voidrender`
- `extra-damage-enchantments:sensitive_to_unravel`
- `extra-damage-enchantments:sensitive_to_herbicide`
- `extra-damage-enchantments:sensitive_to_beastly`
